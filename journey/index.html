Where I’ve Been

A career without straight lines.

The Journey

Different paths. Shared perspective.

A path built through
curiosity.

I've never approached my career as a ladder to climb. It's been more of a collection of experiences, opportunities, and occasionally unexpected directions. Each role brought something different, and none of those lessons stayed confined to the job where I learned them. Looking back, what connects them isn't an industry or a title, but a persistent curiosity about how things work and how they might work better.

2025 — 2026Aubrey, Texas

Mustang Special Utility District

Senior Safety Specialist

Sep 2025 – Oct 2026

Built employee-centered safety infrastructure for a 150-employee water utility, connecting occupational risk, workforce systems, onboarding, and operational readiness.

Selected impact
  • Established a respiratory protection program spanning medical clearance, fit testing, and technical training for approximately 100 operational employees.
  • Traced 100+ missed pre-employment screenings and lapsed medical clearances to systemic gaps across onboarding and vendor transitions; redesigned both workflows to require clearance before Day 1.
  • Unified fragmented reporting forms in a QR-accessible adaptive Jotform with a supervisor response guide; safety-reporting, EHS platform, and uniform program redesigns were projected to save approximately $89.5K annually.
  • Redesigned pre-hire and onboarding with role-specific learning pathways, values-led orientation, and HR, IT, and operations coordination.
  • Supported I-9/E-Verify processing, new-hire orientation, and resolution of administrative and system issues affecting employee readiness.
