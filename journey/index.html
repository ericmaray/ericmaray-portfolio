Where I've Been

A career without straight lines.

A path built through
curiosity.

I've never approached my career as a ladder to climb. It's been more of a collection of experiences, opportunities, and occasionally unexpected directions. Each role brought something different, and none of those lessons stayed confined to the job where I learned them. Looking back, what connects them isn't an industry or a title, but a persistent curiosity about how things work and how they might work better.

2025 — 2026
Aubrey, TX

Senior Safety Specialist / Workforce Operations

Mustang Special Utility District

A role that began in safety and expanded across workforce operations, HR, and organizational development. Developed employee-centered safety initiatives, improved training and reporting systems, strengthened operational processes, and helped connect workforce needs with more sustainable organizational practices.

2014 — 2025 · 11 Years
Corinth, TX

Training & Development / Safety Specialist

CoServ Gas & Electric Cooperative

Eleven years, starting in field operations and moving progressively into safety and L&D work. I stood up the organization's first LMS, managed a $150K+ training budget, built out the leadership development infrastructure the organization hadn't had before, and spent the back half of my time here working on longer-term capability and culture questions.

2006 — 2014 · 8 Years
U.S. & Afghanistan

Staff Sergeant / Geospatial Engineer

United States Army · 101st Airborne Division

Eight years, finishing as Staff Sergeant. Responsible for training compliance and deployment readiness for around 200 soldiers. Two tours in Operation Enduring Freedom. A lot of how I think about accountability, leading under pressure, and what it means to actually be ready — started here.
