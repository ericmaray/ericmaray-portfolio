01 / Safety Communications

Safety Spotlight: Building awareness, one week at a time

Mustang Special Utility District · 2026

I designed Safety Spotlight 2026 as a yearlong communication strategy: 12 monthly safety themes, with weekly messages intended to build on one another. Instead of isolated reminders, the structure connected awareness, practical understanding, reflection, and reinforcement across operational safety, employee well-being, and emerging workplace risks.

04 / Leadership Development

Developing leaders before the promotion

CoServ (Denton County Electric Cooperative) · 2024–2025

In an organization of more than 500 employees, including over 100 in leadership positions, leadership development traditionally began after promotion. While an established program supported newly appointed leaders, there was no structured pathway for employees aspiring to leadership roles. I helped challenge this reactive approach and establish a pre-leadership development cohort, giving more than 30 high-potential employees—nominated by their existing leaders—the opportunity to build foundational leadership capabilities before stepping into formal leadership positions. The initiative shifted the emphasis from developing employees because they had been promoted to preparing them for the responsibilities and expectations of leadership before advancement.

05 / Learning Systems

Standing up an LMS from scratch

CoServ (Denton County Electric Cooperative) · 2024–2025

I played a key role in establishing the organization’s first Learning Management System, helping transition existing training records into a centralized, role-based learning environment. Initially serving as one of two learning administrators for a workforce of more than 500 employees, I later assumed sole responsibility for the platform. My work focused on organizing learning content, developing role-relevant pathways, streamlining compliance training, and improving reporting visibility so employees could access meaningful development opportunities and leaders could better track progress.
