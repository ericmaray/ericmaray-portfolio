Standing up an LMS from scratch

CoServ Gas & Electric · ~500 employees

We didn't have an LMS before this. Rather than build a content library and ask people to find what they needed, I organized the architecture around distinct user groups — so each role landed on content relevant to their work, not just available to everyone. Role-segmented dashboards, automated reporting, SCORM/xAPI deployment across a blended workforce.

02 / Leadership Development

Developing leaders before the promotion

CoServ · Multi-year initiative

The pattern was clear: people were stepping into leadership roles without the foundation they needed, and it was showing in the teams around them. I made the formal case to leadership, earned the buy-in, and built a development track that started before the promotion — so the first day in a new seat wasn't also the first day learning how to lead.

  • →Improved retention and pipeline depth
  • →Role-based, multi-year progression
  • →Built alongside senior leadership

04 / Facilitator Procurement & Experience

Bringing in the right facilitators and setting them up to teach

Denton County Electric Cooperative · Learning & Development

I interviewed and selected external facilitators for their expertise and willingness to tailor sessions to our employees’ work. The procurement and contract arrangements for those engagements accounted for most of the $15,000+ in annual savings I identified.

I built a digital welcome guide that walked facilitators through parking, check-in, classroom setup, and what to expect before and after a session. I arranged lunch options for full-day visits and shared relevant participant feedback with facilitators to help improve future sessions.

05 / Onboarding

Redesigning onboarding around culture and role readiness

At Mustang, I revamped onboarding by correcting outdated pre-hire checklists, building e-learning paths based on role and pay exemption status, and redesigning the orientation experience around the organization’s core values. Instead of leading with rules alone, the new approach connected policies, systems, and expectations to what they meant in practice.

  • →Updated pre-hire checklists that were more than two years out of date
  • →Built role-based learning paths tied to job needs and exemption status
  • →Reframed orientation around Respect, Integrity, Safety, and Innovation
