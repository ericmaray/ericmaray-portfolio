01 / Learning Platform

Standing up an LMS from scratch

CoServ Gas & Electric · ~500 employees

We didn't have an LMS before this. Rather than build a content library and ask people to find what they needed, I organized the architecture around distinct user groups — so each role landed on content relevant to their work, not just available to everyone. Role-segmented dashboards, automated reporting, SCORM/xAPI deployment across a blended workforce.

02 / Leadership Development

Developing leaders before the promotion

CoServ · Multi-year initiative

The pattern was clear: people were stepping into leadership roles without the foundation they needed, and it was showing in the teams around them. I made the formal case to leadership, earned the buy-in, and built a development track that started before the promotion — so the first day in a new seat wasn't also the first day learning how to lead.

  • →Improved retention and pipeline depth
  • →Role-based, multi-year progression
  • →Built alongside senior leadership

03 / Engagement

A weekly safety series, all year

Mustang Special Utility District · 2025–Present

One-off compliance posts disappear. This is a 52-week series where each month builds on the last — treating safety as a habit being formed rather than a topic being covered. Behavior-focused, cumulative, woven into the regular work rhythm rather than added on top of it.

  • →Behavior-focused rather than checklist-focused
  • →Themes that build cumulatively
  • →Integrated into the regular work rhythm

04 / Safety

Four years without a major incident

Zero major OSHA incidents over four consecutive years at a fast-growing utility. The field crews deserve most of that credit. My contribution was helping shift safety training from an annual event into something woven into how people worked day to day.
